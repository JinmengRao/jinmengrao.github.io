The Rising Star in brief

Connecting frontier AI with geographic information science

Jinmeng Rao is a GeoAI researcher and geospatial community builder whose work connects frontier AI with geographic information science. During COVID-19, he co-developed one of the first mobility-based social distancing maps in the United States and a multiscale human mobility flow dataset. This work, published in PNAS, JAMA Network Open, and Scientific Data, has been cited in Nature and PNAS and covered by NBC, FOX, and ABC.

His research on geoprivacy, including LSTM-TrajGAN, CATS, and privacy-preserving GeoAI foundation models, earned the 2024 AAG William L. Garrison Award for Best Dissertation in Computational Geography. In industry, he built multimodal and retrieval-augmented AI for agriculture and Earth observation at Alphabet's Mineral, and he now works on Gemini at Google DeepMind.

He invests heavily in the community. He is Demo Track Co-chair of ACM SIGSPATIAL 2026; an organizer of OASIS 2026, UMFM 2026, and GeoIndustry (2023–2026); an AAG GeoAI symposium organizer since 2022; and co-founder of GISphere, a non-profit that supports GIS students worldwide.

Accomplishments

Four threads of geospatial contribution

Research that has shaped public health response, set the agenda for responsible location data, and helped define the next generation of GeoAI.

01

COVID-19 mobility intelligence for public health

Turned large-scale location data into timely, open evidence for pandemic response.

  • Co-developed one of the first mobility-based social distancing maps in the U.S. (2020), which drew coverage from NBC, FOX, and ABC.
  • Built a multiscale dynamic human mobility flow dataset (Scientific Data, 380+ citations), widely reused in epidemiology and urban research.
  • Co-authored mobility–transmission studies in PNAS (190+ citations) and JAMA Network Open (270+ citations).
  • Cited by high-impact studies in Nature (Chang et al., 2021; Buckee et al., 2021) and PNAS (Thomas et al., 2022). The research was supported by the NSF.
Human mobilityHealth geographyOpen data
02

Geoprivacy and trustworthy GeoAI

Pioneered deep generative approaches that let organizations share and analyze location data while protecting individuals.

  • LSTM-TrajGAN (GIScience 2021, 270+ citations) is a privacy-preserving trajectory generation framework that other researchers have adopted to synthesize trajectory data.
  • CATS (IJGIS, 2023) introduced conditional adversarial trajectory synthesis for privacy-preserving data publication.
  • Led a vision for privacy-preserving and secure GeoAI foundation models (ACM SIGSPATIAL 2023). His federated location-recommendation work has been described as the first to bring federated learning to spatial data science.
  • His dissertation, Trajectory Privacy Protection with GeoAI, received the 2024 AAG William L. Garrison Award.
GeoprivacyGenerative AIFederated learning
03

GeoAI foundation models and spatial intelligence

Helping to define the research agenda for the next generation of geospatial AI.

  • Co-authored “Towards the Next Generation of Geospatial Artificial Intelligence” (2025, 220+ citations), a roadmap for GeoAI in the era of foundation models.
  • Co-developed SRL, a general-purpose framework for spatial representation learning (ACM SIGSPATIAL 2024), and TRAJGANR (2026) for trajectory-centric urban multimodal learning.
  • Co-authored book chapters on the evolution and the ethics of GeoAI, on GeoAI methodological foundations, and on trajectory privacy protection.
  • Publishes across GIScience and AI venues, including PNAS, IJGIS, ACM SIGSPATIAL, GIScience, SIGIR, AAAI, NAACL, and COLM.
Foundation modelsLocation encodingUrban AI
04

From research to industry

Brings rigorous GeoAI thinking to large-scale AI systems in production.

  • At Mineral, Alphabet's computational agriculture moonshot, he built multimodal LLM and retrieval-augmented generation systems for agriculture and Earth observation, including IM-RAG (SIGIR 2024) and Insight Miner.
  • Inventor on U.S. patents and patent applications covering multimodal environmental data fusion for agricultural inference, agricultural insight mining, and plant tracking.
  • At Google DeepMind, he contributes to post-training and evaluation for Gemini 2.5 and Gemini 3. He brings first-hand expertise in frontier models and AI agents back to the geospatial community.

Aspiration
“I want spatial intelligence to be a first-class, trustworthy capability of frontier AI, and I want the geospatial community to lead how it is built and used.”
1

Spatially intelligent AI

Advance geospatial foundation models and AI agents that can reason about places, movement, and the changing Earth, with open benchmarks and challenges to measure progress.

2

Trustworthy GeoAI by default

Make privacy, fairness, and accountability core design principles of geospatial AI, so that location data can serve society without compromising individuals.

3

An open, global community

Keep building bridges between industry and academia, and keep opening doors for students worldwide to join the geospatial field.
