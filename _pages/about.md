I am a **Geospatial AI (GeoAI) researcher** and a **Senior Research Engineer at [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/)**. My work sits at the intersection of frontier AI and geographic information science: I build large-scale AI systems, and I work to bring the latest advances in foundation models, privacy-preserving AI, and agentic AI to the geospatial community.

At Google DeepMind, I am a team member for [Gemini 2.5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.06261) and Gemini 3, focusing on post-training and evaluation for Gemini's long-context, coding, and agentic capabilities. Previously, I was a research scientist at [Mineral](https://x.company/projects/mineral/), Alphabet's computational agriculture moonshot, and an AI resident at [Google \[X\]](https://x.company), where I worked on multimodal LLMs and retrieval-augmented generation for agriculture and earth observation.

I received my Ph.D. in Geographic Information Science and my M.S. in Computer Sciences from the [University of Wisconsin-Madison](https://www.wisc.edu/), advised by [Prof. Song Gao](https://geography.wisc.edu/staff/gao-song/) in the [Geospatial Data Science Lab](https://geography.wisc.edu/geods/). My dissertation, *Trajectory Privacy Protection with Geospatial Artificial Intelligence*, received the **2024 AAG William L. Garrison Award for Best Dissertation in Computational Geography**. Before that, I received my B.S. and M.S. in GIS from [Wuhan University](https://en.whu.edu.cn/).

**Research.** My geospatial research spans GeoAI, geoprivacy and trajectory privacy (e.g., [LSTM-TrajGAN](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.GIScience.2021.I.12), CATS, privacy-preserving and secure GeoAI foundation models), human mobility, and computational agriculture. During COVID-19, I co-developed one of the first mobility-based social distancing maps in the U.S. and a multiscale human mobility flow dataset. This work, published in *PNAS*, *JAMA Network Open*, and *Scientific Data*, has been widely used in public health research and covered by NBC, FOX, and ABC.

**Community.** I am deeply engaged in the geospatial community. I serve as **Demo Track Co-chair of [ACM SIGSPATIAL 2026](https://sigspatial2026.sigspatial.org/organizers.html)**. I also co-organize several SIGSPATIAL events: the [OASIS 2026](https://rsvp.withgoogle.com/events/oasis-2026/) student challenge on open agents with spatial intelligence for social good, the [UMFM 2026](https://rsvp.withgoogle.com/events/sigspatial-2026-umfm-workshop) workshop on urban mobility foundation models, and the [GeoIndustry](https://geoindustry.github.io/geoindustryworkshop2026/) workshop series (2023&ndash;2026). In addition, I have organized GeoAI symposium sessions at the AAG Annual Meeting every year since 2022 (as symposium co-chair since 2023), and I co-founded [GISphere](https://gisphere.info/), a non-profit that helps students worldwide pursue GIS education.
